About us

The Golden Table opened in 2015 with one idea: generous, honest cooking that people want to share.

Every morning our team buys fresh produce from nearby farms and builds the menu around what is best that day. Come for a lunch with friends, a family dinner or a special occasion. There is always a seat for you.
